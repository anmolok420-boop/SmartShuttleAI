ETA model notes

How the prediction pipeline works

  1. GPS cleaningReject implausible jumps and smooth coordinate noise.
  2. Map matchingSnap accepted readings to the nearest campus route segment.
  3. ETA estimationCombine distance, speed, dwell time, delay and confidence.
  4. Dropout recoveryUse dead reckoning for temporary GPS interruptions.
Performance figures must be validated against actual bus arrival timestamps. The current campus feed is simulated and must not be presented as field-validated accuracy.
